Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about CHRVA Adult Volleyball, including registration, memberships, tournaments, rosters, payments, and eligibility.

Getting Started

How do I register for a CHRVA adult tournament?
Registration can be made through AES or through the One Day Team Form Information on how to create a club/team in AES can be found on the AES How To Page Tournament registration details will be listed on the main Adult Volleyball page and/or the event page.
Do I need a USAV/CHRVA membership to play?
Yes. All CHRVA adult events require players to have a valid USAV membership. One Day Memberships and coaching memberships count as valid USAV Memberships. If getting a one-day membership, be sure to put the proper event date.

Memberships can be acquired by going to the Adult Memberships Page
Can players from another USAV region participate?
Yes, players with a valid USAV membership from another region may participate in CHRVA events. However, certain CHRVA-specific eligibility benefits require CHRVA membership.

Tournaments

Where can I find the adult tournament schedule?
The adult tournament schedule will be posted on the Website. Events may also be shared through CHRVA communications and social media.
When is payment due?
Payment deadlines may vary by event, but teams should expect payment to be due the Wednesday before the tournament. Teams that have not completed payment by the listed deadline may not be guaranteed a spot.
Are jerseys required?
For CHRVA events, numbers ARE required, however matching jerseys are not. You are allowed to tape or write numbers on shirts. The libero jersey should be distinctly different but you will not be penalized if it is not.
What happens if a tournament is canceled?
If an event is canceled, teams will be notified as soon as possible. Refund or credit information will be communicated directly to affected teams based on the event circumstances. Teams that have registered AND paid will get credit towards Nationals Reimbursement and Regionals Eligibility.

Rosters & Eligibility

Do we need to submit a roster?
Teams only need to submit rosters ahead of time for Regionals and Nationals. All other tournaments, the Tournament Director will have previous rosters or blank rosters the Team Rep can fill out day of.
Can we add or change players after registering?
Generally, yes. Player changes may not be allowed depending on the event and roster deadline such as for Nationals or Regionals. Teams should contact their Division Coordinator or event contact as early as possible if roster changes are needed.
What is a One-Day Team?
A One-Day Team is a flexible registration option for a single event. This option may be useful for teams or players who are not registering as a full CHRVA adult team for the season. For more details see One Day Teams & Memberships
What is a Conglomerate Team?
A conglomerate team is basically an adult age team. The National Office recognizes teams that start at 40+ and on up in increments of five years. What does that mean? If you will turn 40 sometime during 2023, you are eligible to play on a 40+ team, as are all players older than that. But, if you are 40, you are not eligible to play on a 45+, 50+, and so on.

Why play on a conglomerate team? Some players only do so at Nationals. Others want to play more often so will be on a regular divisional team and an age team. You can also play on two conglomerate teams if you qualify for both age groups. If you do that, you cannot play on a divisional team, as a player can be on no more than two teams. CHRVA recognizes what the National Office does, and also includes 30+ and 35+. Age teams can play in any tournament, but generally chooses a division.
