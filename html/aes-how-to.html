AES How-To Guide

Use these guides to set up your AES account, create club teams, and register for CHRVA tournaments.

Open AES

Create an AES Account, Club, and Teams

Click on any of the zoomed out images to expand them in a different tab.

  • Use the "Open AES" link at the top.
  • In the new page, click sign in on the top right corner.
  • You should see the page to the right, click "Create Account"
  • On the following page, enter the details for your account
  • Press "Add Your Profile" which will create a SportsEngine Profile
  • You then need to verify your email

After you verify your email, now you need to create an AES Profile

  • Enter your birth date, gender, email and phone
  • Check Club Director on the bottom left
  • Click "Create"

Now that your AES Profile has been created, you need to add your Club details. USAV requires all teams to be part of a club but if you only plan on having one team, you can name your club the same as your team.

  • Fill in Club Name
  • Give yourself a unique 5 digit Club Code
  • Use Adult Volleyball for Club Type
  • Use Chesapeake (CH) for USAV Region
  • Click "Continue"

Add a few more club details (Don't worry, you won't have to do this ever again)

  • Fill in your email
  • Fill in your Address and all required fields (marked with a red asterisk *)
  • Click "Create Club"

You should now see the page to your right which is your club home page. From here you can add players, add teams, and register for tournaments. If you need help with any of that visit our next AES How-To section, "Teams & Tournament Registration" above!.

Create a Team and Register for Tournaments

This guide will walk you through adding players, creating teams and registering for tournaments

  • From your Club Home page, click "Players" on the left
  • On the bottom right, click "Add Player"

Player lookup does NOT work.
Click "Create New"

Add all the player details here.

  • Enter First Name
  • Enter Last Name
  • Enter Birth Date
  • Enter Gender
  • If you plan on going to USAV Nationals, enter the player's USAV Number and jersey
  • If you have multiple players to create, click "Create More Players" otherwise, click "Create Player"
  • From your Club Home page, click "Teams" on the left
  • On the bottom right, click "Add Team"
  • Choose the Gender of the team
  • Choose the Division of the team
  • Assign the Team Rank, most likely just 1. This has to be different than any other teams, if any, you have in the club
  • Choose the Team Name
  • Click "Create Team" or if you want to create multiple, click "Create More Teams"
  • From your Club Home Page, click "Register" on the left
  • At the time of making this tutorial, no events have been published in AES yet. We will update this tutorial with actual events once the events get published.
